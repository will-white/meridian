## 2. Rules

- **Cohorts.** With 98 data years and 30 plan years there are `98 − 30 + 1 = 69` complete cohorts. Path `k` (0 to 68) starts at data row `k` (start year 1928 + `k`); plan year `t` uses data row `k + t`. Stocks, bonds and inflation come from the same row, as they happened.
- **No tax.** The person is past 59½ (July 2021) in every year and the Roth was funded before the plan (it counts as first funded in 2022, so the five-year clock is met): every withdrawal is tax-free and penalty-free. A Roth has no RMD.
- **The year.** At the start of year `t` the need `40,000 × cpi[t]` is withdrawn (`cpi[0] = 1`). If the balance `B` is at least the need, `B ← B − need`; otherwise the year funds only `B`, its shortfall is `need − B`, `B ← 0`, and the path has failed (the first such year is its failing plan-year index). The account is then rebalanced to its mix and earns the year's return: `B ← B × (1 + s × stocks + (1 − s) × bonds)`, with `s` the stock share (0.5 or 0.75) and the rates of the year's row. Then `cpi[t+1] = cpi[t] × (1 + inflation)` of the same row. A failed path keeps running with nothing to draw: every later year's whole need is shortfall.
- **Outputs per path.** Funded or failed; the failing plan-year index (0 = 2027); the lifetime shortfall in today's dollars, `Σ shortfall_t ÷ cpi[t]`; ending wealth, nominal (the balance at the end of 2056) and in today's dollars (÷ `cpi[30]`).
- **Funded count** = paths that never fail; `successBp = floor(funded × 10000 ÷ paths)` (64 of 69 → 9275; 65 of 69 → 9420).
- **Ranking and percentiles.** Paths are ordered by ending wealth in today's dollars in cents ascending, then by the year the money ran out ascending with "never" last, then by path index. The path at percentile `p` is the one at rank `max(0, ceil(p × N ÷ 100) − 1)`; with `N = 69`: p10 → rank 6, p50 → rank 34, p90 → rank 62. The reported percentile of ending wealth is that path's value.
- **The Trinity convention** (for the comparison with the published study only): start years 1928 to 1966 (39 windows of 30 years that end by 1995); each year the balance first earns the year's return, then `40,000 × the price level at that year's end` is withdrawn (`cpi` after the year's inflation); a window fails when a withdrawal exceeds the balance.
- **Rounding.** Dollar figures are the float64 results rounded half away from zero to the cent.
