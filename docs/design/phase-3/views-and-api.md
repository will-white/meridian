# Phase 3 design: Views, API and web (Investments)

Area: the five investment views of the spec (Portfolio, Allocation, Holdings, Performance, Income) as API contracts, services and React pages; asset classes, blended-fund splits, household targets, drift and the rebalance suggestion; holdings across accounts with cost-basis coverage, the weighted expense ratio and concentration warnings; performance versus a benchmark (presented over the lots & performance engine); dividend and interest income by month with the optional feed into the plan's income bucket; the account fidelity badge; the dashboard's investment blocks; the viewer role; and the two owner-trust screens that operationalize the Phase 3 gate: *reconcile to a statement* (saved per account and date) and *why did it change* (price effect, quantity effect, flows, income, residual).

Angle: owner-first. Every value says *as of when* and *from what source*; every change decomposes; every account can be checked against a statement and the check is kept; every suggestion states its arithmetic; nothing is executed against an institution (the app is read-only toward them). The API is derived from the screens.

This is the merged area design. Design A is the base; the grafts both judges asked for from Design B are folded in (residual cash as a cash row, the snapshot-beats-older-price valuation rule with an implied price, reinvested dividends as income with a `reinvested` flag, exclusion of feed rows from Phase 1/2 matching, `use_cash` and `inAccount` in the rebalance, a tax rank for treatment `none`, positions staleness separate from price staleness, a minimum-trade setting, `flowsSource`, the securities list for the pickers, the MWR annualization note, the primary-class split rule); every flaw either judge named is resolved in the text, and §11 records what was taken from where and the one judge suggestion that was overruled. Every number in §7 was re-derived with a BigInt reference script (`scratchpad/views/check.mjs`, scratch only; nothing in the repo was modified).

Source of truth: the design spec; `docs/phase-1-plan.md` and `docs/phase-2-plan.md` are binding for conventions. The two sibling Phase 3 area designs already in `docs/design/phase-3/` (`imports-and-data.md`, `lots-and-performance.md`) own the tables and the engine this area reads; §9 states every interface this area assumes of them and names the places where those two documents disagree with each other, so the Phase 3 plan can reconcile the seams once. This document is self-contained: an implementer builds this area from it.

**Spec conflict flagged up front.** The spec's data-sources table marks OFX/QFX as phase 3 while its build-phase list puts OFX under Phase 6 Polish. This design treats OFX as out of scope for Phase 3; nothing here depends on it, and every view reads `holding_snapshot` / `investment_txn` without caring which adapter wrote them, so a later OFX adapter feeds the same pipeline (Deviation 1).

Conventions carried from Phases 1 and 2: TypeScript strict with `exactOptionalPropertyTypes` and `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`; NodeNext imports with `.js`; Zod contracts in `packages/shared/src/api.ts` shared by API and web; cents as `CentsString`; dates `YYYY-MM-DD`; months `YYYY-MM`; services in `packages/db/src/services` taking `DbOrTx`; `PlanError(status, code, message)` mapped by `usePlanErrors`; one audit batch per request through `writeAudit` (never when before equals after); `today` from the client within ±2 days of the household date (`resolveToday`); bigint division truncates toward zero.

## 1. Decisions (binding)

1. **Numeric representation.** Share quantities and prices are `Fixed8` (`bigint` × 10⁸, the exact image of `numeric(20,8)`; parsed from the driver's string with `parseFixed8`, never through `Number`). On the wire they are decimal strings with at most eight fraction digits (`Fixed8String`, the same form the imports and engine areas use: `"14.0544"` or `"14.05440000"`; readers parse, never compare text). Cents stay `bigint` / `CentsString`. Rates that come from the engine (TWR, XIRR) are JSON numbers; ratios this area derives from cents (weights, drift, gain %, expense ratios) are integer basis points or parts per million. Reason: one representation across the four Phase 3 areas; no float ever touches a quantity, a price or a cent.
2. **Rounding: the engine's three conversions round half away from zero; everything else truncates.** `mulToCents(q, p) = roundHalfAway(q × p, 10¹⁴)` (R1), `quantityForCents(c, p) = roundHalfAway(c × 10¹⁴, p)` (R2) and `impliedPrice(c, q) = roundHalfAway(|c| × 10¹⁴, |q|)` (R3) are taken from the shared Fixed8 module exactly as the lots & performance area defines them, so a holding valued here equals the same holding valued by the engine to the cent. Every ratio this area computes itself (`bp(part, total) = truncDiv(part × 10000, total)`, class splits, target cents, pro-rata gain estimates, weighted ppm) uses Phase 2's truncation. Reason: stated once, used everywhere, hand-checkable; brokerages round market value to the nearest cent and the gate compares against their statements.
3. **An account's value is its latest `balance_snapshot` on or before `asOf`, whatever the source.** This is the number Phase 2's net worth already uses, so the dashboard, the Accounts page and the Portfolio always agree. Holdings *explain* that value; they never replace it. The prices & valuation area writes `source = 'computed'` rows (holdings marked to market plus carried cash) that never overwrite a reported row (`ON CONFLICT (account_id, date) DO UPDATE … WHERE balance_snapshot.source = 'computed'`); the sync's `simplefin` upsert overwrites unconditionally, as today. Reason: one source of truth for "what is this account worth"; no second valuation path for net worth.
4. **Cash is a holding, and the residual is cash too.** A source that reports cash (SimpleFIN `extra`, a Fidelity positions file with SPAXX) gives a `holding_snapshot` row of the canonical cash security (`security.type = 'cash'`, one per currency, created by the imports area), valued at 1.00000000 per unit. For every account, `residualCents = accountValue − Σ holding values` (securities plus reported cash). A positive residual is a per-account **"Cash (unlisted)"** line in class `cash` (the common SimpleFIN case, where cash is outside the positions list); a negative one is a `negative_residual` warning on the account, excluded from allocation and shown on the reconcile screen. Reason: allocation sums to the portfolio total exactly, the rebalance can use idle cash, and the reconcile screen's residual means "cash and other, not in the positions list" rather than silently losing it.
5. **A holding is valued by the statement unless a strictly newer price exists.** For `(account, security)` with latest snapshot `s` on or before `asOf` and latest price `p` on or before `asOf`: if `p` exists and `p.date > s.date` → `mulToCents(s.quantity, p.close)` with `priceSource = p.source` (`provider | implied | manual`); otherwise the snapshot's own `value_cents` with `price = impliedPrice(value, quantity)` and `priceSource = 'snapshot'`. A dollar-only position (`quantity NULL`) is always its snapshot value (`priceSource = 'snapshot'`, flag `dollarOnly`). Cash is always quantity × 1. Reason: on the statement date the app shows the brokerage's own number (the gate), and a newer close updates it afterwards; an equal-dated implied price never overrides what the statement said.
6. **Asset classes and blended funds.** `AssetClass = us_stock | intl_stock | bonds | cash | real_estate | other`. `security.asset_class` (the imports area's column) is the primary class; optional `security_allocation` rows `(security_id, asset_class, weight_bp)` with Σ = 10 000 split a blended fund, and the primary class must be one of the split classes (it receives the truncation remainder). No rows → 100 % primary. Edited by the owner on the Holdings page (`PUT /api/investments/securities/:id/allocation`), audited as entity `security`. Reason: the common case stays one column; a target-date fund is a few rows, not a schema change.
7. **Household targets and bands.** `allocation_target(asset_class PK, target_bp)` (the imports area's table and `PUT /api/investments/allocation-targets` route: either empty or a set summing to 10 000 bp; absent classes are 0). `household_setting.rebalance_band_bp` (default 500 = 5 pp) is the drift band and `household_setting.rebalance_min_trade_cents` (default 10 000 = $100) the smallest suggested trade; both are edited on the Allocation page through `PATCH /api/settings`. Reason: household facts must agree across devices and have one owner each.
8. **Allocation is over classified value.** Accounts at fidelity `positions` or `full_history` are classified; `balance_only` accounts are listed as *unclassified* with their value and share of the total but excluded from the class percentages, the drift and the rebalance. Reason: the spec puts allocation under the positions level; folding an unknown 401(k) into "other" would make every drift number wrong.
9. **Rebalance suggestion** (algorithm in §3.7): triggered when any class's |drift| ≥ band. Target cents per class truncate with the remainder on the largest-target class so targets sum to the classified value; deltas (target − current) are satisfied account by account in tax-rank order `pre_tax = hsa = roth (0) < none (1) < taxable (2)`, then value desc, then name, then id; inside an account sells fund buys exactly (money never crosses accounts); only *pure* positions (100 % one class) and the cash line are traded (blended funds are held constant); selling the cash line is `use_cash`, buying cash is `hold_cash`; a buy of a class the account does not hold names the household's largest pure holding of that class with `inAccount: false` ("VXUS or the plan's equivalent"); trades under the minimum are removed by a stated pass; whatever remains unmet is `residual` ("needs money moved between accounts" or "held only through blended funds"). Taxable sells carry a pro-rata estimated gain when cost basis is known. Pure and deterministic. Reason: the spec's "prefers tax-advantaged accounts for trades", with every rule a household can follow by hand.
10. **Holdings rows** are per security across accounts, expandable per account. Quantity and value come from the latest statement (D5) when one exists; cost basis and lot counts come from the lots & performance engine's `computeHoldings` for accounts with trade history; coverage is `full | partial | none` per security; `gainBp = bp(gain, coveredCost)`. When the engine's lots disagree with the statement (`position_mismatch`), the covered cost basis is still shown with a `lots_mismatch` warning rather than hidden. Weighted expense ratio = `truncDiv(Σ value × ppm, Σ value)` over covered rows where a null ratio counts as 0 for types `stock | bond | cash | cd` and as unknown (excluded from both sums) for `etf | fund | other`; `coveredBp` says how much of the portfolio the number covers; `annualCostCents = truncDiv(Σ value × ppm, 1 000 000)`. Concentration: an individual stock ≥ 1 000 bp of the classified total → `warn`, ≥ 2 500 bp → `bad`; any one fund/ETF ≥ 6 000 bp → `info`. Reason: integer math on the imports area's `expense_ratio_ppm`, and warnings that say what they mean.
11. **Fidelity is derived, never stored.** For investment-type accounts (`brokerage, 401k, ira, roth, hsa, 529`): `hasTrades` (any `buy | sell | reinvest | split` row or a securities `transfer`, the imports area's rule) → candidate `full_history`; if the engine's position check against the latest `holding_snapshot` reports a `position_mismatch`, the badge is `positions` with `fidelityNote` = "trade history incomplete: VTI lots cover 20 of 40 shares"; else `full_history` (with the note "no statement to check against" when the account has no snapshot). Otherwise `positions` when any `holding_snapshot` exists, else `balance_only`; `null` for other types. Reason: the spec says each account "shows which level it has", and "full history" must mean lots explain the statement, not that a dividend row exists.
12. **Performance is a presentation of the engine.** `GET /api/investments/performance` calls the lots & performance area's `getPerformance` (true TWR for full-history accounts, Modified Dietz for the others, Excel-exact XIRR, start-of-day flows, opening-value flows, benchmark shadow portfolio, household total with internal flows dropped) and adds only what the page needs: the period presets, bp-rounded chart series (growth of 10 000 at the engine's sub-period boundaries, benchmark index), `flowsSource` per row, the uncategorized-row count and the readable reason strings. No second returns engine exists. Periods: `1m | 3m | ytd | 1y | 3y | 5y | all | custom` with the engine's semantics (`V(from)` = end of day on `from`, flows in `(from, to]`, `ytd` from Dec 31, `all` = since inception). Reason: one implementation of every rate; the spec's "money-weighted (XIRR) and time-weighted (comparable to a benchmark)" is exactly what the engine returns.
13. **Benchmark** = `household_setting.benchmark_security_id` (set in Settings, chosen from `GET /api/investments/securities`), overridable per request. The page shows the engine's benchmark TWR (price return of the series the prices area supplies, labelled `priceOnly` until adjusted closes exist) and the *benchmark-equivalent ending value* (`shadowValueCents`: the start value and every flow bought into the benchmark at that day's close). Reason: "had every dollar gone into VTI you would have $47,455.24 instead of $45,152.00" is the sentence an owner understands.
14. **Investment income** = `investment_txn` rows of type `dividend` and `interest`, plus `reinvest` rows whose `amount_cents > 0` (the single-row DRIP convention), **unioned** with ledger rows in investment accounts categorized in the household's investment-income category that are linked to no `investment_txn`. A row is `reinvested` when it is a positive `reinvest` row or a `dividend` with a `reinvest` row for the same account, security and date; `reinvestedCents` is a sub-total per month. Kind: `interest` rows, and `dividend` rows on securities of type `cash | cd | bond`, are interest; everything else is dividends. Reason: both reinvest conventions in the sibling designs count the income exactly once, and a SimpleFIN-only account whose dividends were categorized by a rule still appears.
15. **Income feed into the plan.** `account.investment_income_feed ∈ off | link | create` and `household_setting.investment_income_category_id` (an income-bucket category). The feed never links rows itself: the imports area's `linkToLedger` / `linkUnlinkedInvestmentTxns` is the one linker (same account, equal amount, ±3 days, nearest). `link`: every income row linked to a bank row sets that row's category to the feed category unless `category_locked`; an unlinked row is `awaiting`. `create`: as `link`, plus an unlinked row gets a synthetic ledger row (`source = 'investment'`, `external_id = inv:<investment_txn.id>`, the feed category, `is_transfer = false`) linked to it; an **upgrade pass** replaces a synthetic row with the bank's own row when one appears later (the owner's locked category travels to the bank row). `off` deletes the account's unlocked synthetic rows; links on bank rows stay. Reinvested dividends are never fed (no cash arrived). The plan's actual loaders read rows in the feed category from any account, on or off budget (one clause added to the imports area's `on_budget = true` filter), so a fed dividend is plan income with no other change to Phase 2. Synthetic rows and rows linked to an income `investment_txn` are excluded from transfer pairing and recurring detection. Reason: exactly one ledger row per dividend (the bank's or ours, never both), a dividend is never a contribution, and a $24.55 dividend can no longer pair with an unrelated −$24.55.
16. **Reconciliation record.** `GET /api/investments/accounts/:id/reconcile?date=&statementCents=` returns the app's value on that date, the holdings × prices behind it, the cash line, the residual and the difference; `POST` stores an `account_reconciliation` row (audited). The Phase 3 gate becomes a saved fact per account and statement date; the dashboard counts accounts whose latest statement has no record.
17. **"Why did it change."** `GET /api/investments/accounts/:id/changes?from=&to=` attributes Δvalue per holding: `priceEffect = mulToCents(qFrom, pTo) − valueFrom`, `quantityEffect = valueTo − mulToCents(qFrom, pTo)` (they sum exactly to the holding's Δ), plus the residual's Δ, the cash flows and income in the window. The Portfolio's day change and period breakdown use the same function.
18. **Dashboard.** `netWorth` keeps Phase 2's logic and gains a breakdown by account type: `investmentsCents` (brokerage, 401k, ira, roth, hsa, 529), `cashCents` (checking, savings), `debtCents` (credit, loan: signed, normally ≤ 0), `otherCents` (asset); `todayCents` is their sum. A nullable `investments` block carries the total, day change, stale accounts, fidelity counts, securities needing review and the awaiting-reconcile count.
19. **Viewer role.** `AuthStatus` gains `role`; the web hides every editor for viewers; the API relies on the auth plugin (non-GET → 403 `viewer`); no Phase 3 route of this area is `readOnly` (every read is a GET).
20. **Staleness, three kinds.** `priceStale`: price date more than 7 days before `asOf` (the engine's `stale_price` rule); `valuationStale`: the account's latest `balance_snapshot` more than 7 days before `asOf`; `positionsStale`: the latest `holding_snapshot` more than 35 days before `asOf`. Every value is shown with its date.
21. **today / asOf.** `today` as Phase 2. `asOf` defaults to the latest `balance_snapshot.date ≤ today` across the selected investment accounts (null when none → empty states) and may be given explicitly (`?asOf=`, any date ≤ today, so a statement from two weeks ago can be checked). Investment accounts = open accounts of the six investment types; closed ones only with `includeClosed=true`.
22. **No caches, one job.** Views are computed per request (`Cache-Control: no-store`); the only worker job this area owns is `investments.income_feed`.
23. **OFX out of scope** for Phase 3 (the build list is the schedule; conflict flagged above and in §8).
24. **Web layout.** One nav entry "Investments" with sub-routes `/investments` (Portfolio), `/investments/allocation`, `/investments/holdings`, `/investments/performance`, `/investments/income` (the imports area's `/investments/securities`, `/investments/accounts/:id` and `/investments/transactions` share the shell); period, dates and `asOf` in the URL; ECharts through the existing `EChart` wrapper (adds `BarChart`, `LegendComponent`); fixed palette slots per asset class in every chart.
25. **Audit.** `security` (class and splits), `allocation_target` (the imports area's service), `household_setting` (the new columns through the existing service), `account` (only `investment_income_feed`), `account_reconciliation` (create, update, delete). One batch per request; nothing written when before equals after.

## 2. Data model

Money columns are `bigint` cents; quantities and prices `numeric(20,8)`. Tables and columns owned by this area are given in full. Columns of the sibling areas' tables that the views read are listed with the shape assumed (full statement in §9). Drizzle definitions go in `packages/db/src/schema/investments.ts` (co-owned by the four areas) except the two existing tables, which change in `accounts.ts` and `plan.ts`; `CHECK`s use `check()` from `drizzle-orm/pg-core` (0.38.4) and every CHECK is mirrored by a service validation with a named error code.

### 2.1 `security_allocation` (new, this area)
```sql
CREATE TABLE "security_allocation" (
  "security_id" uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES "security"("id") ON DELETE CASCADE,
  "asset_class" text NOT NULL,
  "weight_bp"   integer NOT NULL,
  CONSTRAINT "security_allocation_pk" PRIMARY KEY ("security_id", "asset_class"),
  CONSTRAINT "security_allocation_class_check" CHECK ("asset_class" IN ('us_stock','intl_stock','bonds','cash','real_estate','other')),
  CONSTRAINT "security_allocation_weight_check" CHECK ("weight_bp" BETWEEN 1 AND 10000)
);
```
Invariants (service `setSecurityAllocation`, one transaction, full replace): per security the rows sum to exactly 10 000 or there are none (`400 split_sum`); when rows exist, `security.asset_class` is one of the split classes (`400 split_primary`); a `cash`-type security cannot be split (`400 cash_security_reserved`). Drizzle: `securityAllocation`.

### 2.2 `account_reconciliation` (new, this area)
```sql
CREATE TABLE "account_reconciliation" (
  "id"               uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid() NOT NULL,
  "account_id"       uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES "account"("id") ON DELETE CASCADE,
  "date"             date NOT NULL,
  "statement_cents"  bigint NOT NULL,
  "app_value_cents"  bigint,            -- the app's value on that date when recorded; null when it had none
  "difference_cents" bigint,            -- app_value − statement; null when app_value is null
  "note"             text,
  "created_by"       uuid REFERENCES "user"("id") ON DELETE SET NULL,
  "created_at"       timestamp with time zone DEFAULT now() NOT NULL
);
CREATE UNIQUE INDEX "account_reconciliation_account_date_idx" ON "account_reconciliation" ("account_id", "date");
```
One record per account and statement date; re-recording the same date replaces it (upsert, audited as `update`). Drizzle: `accountReconciliation`.

### 2.4 `account` (Phase 1 table, one column added)
```sql
ALTER TABLE "account" ADD COLUMN "investment_income_feed" text DEFAULT 'off' NOT NULL;
```

### 2.5 `investment_txn` (imports area's table; one index requested here)

```sql
CREATE INDEX "investment_txn_account_type_date_idx" ON "investment_txn" ("account_id", "type", "date");
```

### 2.7 Columns read from the sibling areas (assumed; §9 has the full seam)
- `security`: `id`, `ticker text NULL`, `name`, `type ∈ stock|etf|fund|bond|cash|cd|other`, `asset_class` (primary, D6), `expense_ratio_ppm integer NULL`, `currency`, `needs_review`, `archived`.
- `price`: `security_id`, `date`, `close numeric(20,8)`, `source ∈ provider|implied|manual` (the engine area also lists `provider_adjusted`, `csv`); unique `(security_id, date)`. Cash securities have no price rows.
- `holding_snapshot`: `account_id`, `security_id`, `date`, `quantity numeric(20,8) NULL` (null = dollar-only), `price numeric(20,8) NULL`, `value_cents bigint NOT NULL`, `reported_cost_basis_cents NULL`, `source ∈ simplefin|csv|manual`; unique `(account_id, security_id, date)`. A snapshot *set* is all rows with one `(account_id, date)`.
- `investment_txn`: `id`, `account_id`, `security_id NULL`, `date`, `sequence`, `type ∈ buy|sell|dividend|interest|reinvest|fee|split|transfer`, `quantity`, `price`, `amount_cents` (signed cash effect: dividend/interest ≥ 0; a `reinvest` row is ≤ 0 under the imports area's two-row convention and = the dividend amount under the engine area's one-row convention, see D14), `ledger_transaction_id`, `raw_description`.
- `lot` is **not read directly**: cost basis per `(account, security)` comes from the engine's `computeHoldings` (§9), which is the only reader of lot rows.
- `allocation_target(asset_class PK, target_bp)`; `balance_snapshot` (Phase 1) with `source = 'computed'` rows written by the valuation area (D3).

### 2.8 Migration `0002_phase3_investments` steps (this area's fragment of the shared Phase 3 migration)

## 3. Algorithms

Pure engines live in `packages/shared/src/investmentViews.ts` (isomorphic, no DB, no clock, deterministic for any input order) and are driven by loaders in `packages/db/src/services/investmentViews.ts`. Every worked example uses the seed of §7.0 (today 2026-10-14; `asOf` 2026-10-13; accounts B "Vanguard Brokerage" (taxable, full history, SimpleFIN) and R "Fidelity Roth IRA" (roth, positions, CSV); securities VTI, VXUS, BND, AAPL, the ticker-less TR2050 (split 54/36/10 us/intl/bonds) and the cash security). Seed totals as of 2026-10-13: B value 2,705,200 = securities 2,562,000 (VTI 1,250,000, VXUS 712,000, AAPL 600,000) + residual cash 143,200; R value 1,820,000 = VTI 625,000 + BND 748,000 + TR2050 420,000 + cash 27,000; household 4,525,200.

### 3.1 Fixed8 helpers used by this area (`packages/shared` Fixed8 module, §9)
```ts
parseFixed8(s: string): Fixed8; formatFixed8(v: Fixed8): string;          // the shared module's forms
roundHalfAway(n: bigint, d: bigint): bigint;                             // d > 0n asserted
mulToCents(q: Fixed8, p: Fixed8): bigint;                                 // R1: roundHalfAway(q × p, 10n ** 14n)
quantityForCents(cents: bigint, p: Fixed8): Fixed8;                       // R2: roundHalfAway(cents × 10n ** 14n, p); p > 0n
impliedPrice(cents: bigint, q: Fixed8): Fixed8;                           // R3: roundHalfAway(|cents| × 10n ** 14n, |q|); q ≠ 0n
// this area's own (investmentViews.ts)
bp(part: bigint, total: bigint): number | null;                           // total <= 0n → null; Number(truncDiv(part * 10000n, total))
ppmWeighted(rows: Array<{ valueCents: bigint; ppm: number | null; type: SecurityType }>): { ppm: number | null; coveredCents: bigint; totalCents: bigint; annualCostCents: bigint };
splitValue(valueCents: bigint, splits: Array<{ assetClass: AssetClass; weightBp: number }>, primary: AssetClass): Map<AssetClass, bigint>;
```

### 3.2 Loaders (`services/investmentViews.ts`, all `DbOrTx`, read-only)
`investmentContext(db, { today, asOf?, accountIds?, includeClosed? })` runs one round of queries and returns everything the views share:
- `accounts`: open accounts of the six investment types (closed only on request), ordered by name, id; each with `taxTreatment`, `onBudget`, `closed`, `connectionType`.

- `asOf` (when not given): `max(valuations.date)`; null when no account has a snapshot.
- `snapshots`: for each account, the latest snapshot *set* on or before `asOf` (`DISTINCT ON (account_id)` over `holding_snapshot` dates, then all rows of that `(account_id, date)`), each row with `quantity`, `price`, `value_cents`, `reported_cost_basis_cents`, `source`.
- `prices`: `DISTINCT ON (security_id)` latest `price` on or before `asOf` for every security in the snapshots plus the benchmark → `{ close, date, source }`.
- `securities`: the rows referenced, with their `security_allocation` splits.
- `engineHoldings`: the engine's `computeHoldings(db, { accountIds: full-history candidates, asOf })` → per `(account, security)` `{ quantity, costBasisCents, unrealizedCents, unknownBasisQuantity, lotCount, stale }` (seam §9).
- `fidelity`: §3.5. `targets`, `setting` (band, minimum trade, benchmark, feed category).
Every view is then a pure function of this context. Seven to nine queries per request; no N+1.

### 3.7 Rebalance suggestion (`suggestRebalance`, D9)
Input: `{ accounts: [{ id, name, taxTreatment, valueCents, lines: [{ securityId, ticker, type, assetClass, splits, valueCents, unrealizedGainCents | null, kind: 'position' | 'cash' | 'residual' }] }], targetsBp, bandBp, minTradeCents, prices (close per security), householdPure: Map<AssetClass, { securityId, ticker }> }` (the household's largest pure holding per class).
1. `current`, `C`, `targetCents`, `delta[c] = targetCents[c] − current[c]` as §3.6 (Σ delta = 0). No targets → `null`. No class out of band → `{ triggered: false, trades: [] }`.
2. Tradable lines: pure positions (no splits) and the cash lines (reported cash and residual, merged per account as one `cash` line). Blended funds are never traded.
3. Accounts ordered by rank `pre_tax 0, hsa 0, roth 0, none 1, taxable 2`, then `valueCents` desc, then name, then id.
4. For each account `a`:
   - `over` = classes with `delta < 0`, most negative first (ties: enum order); `under` = classes with `delta > 0`, largest first.
   - `cap[c] = min(Σ a's tradable value of class c, −delta[c])` for `c ∈ over`; `sellAvail = Σ cap`; `buyAvail = Σ_{under} delta`; `amount = min(sellAvail, buyAvail)`; `amount ≤ 0` → next account.
   - Sells: walk `over`; `s = min(cap[c], left)`; within the class, lines by value desc (ties: ticker, id); each trade takes `min(line value, s)`; `delta[c] += taken`. A cash line's sale is action `use_cash`; a position's is `sell` with `approxQuantity = quantityForCents(amount, close)`.
   - Buys: walk `under`; `b = min(delta[c], left)`; `delta[c] −= b`. Class `cash` → action `hold_cash`. Otherwise the trade names the account's largest pure position of class `c` (`inAccount: true`), else `householdPure[c]` with `inAccount: false` and the note "<ticker> or the plan's equivalent <class> fund (held in <account>)", else `securityId null` with "a <class> fund (new position)"; `approxQuantity` when a security and a price are known.
   - Taxable `sell` with known gain: `estimatedGainCents = truncDiv(unrealizedGainCents × amount, lineValue)` and the note "may realize about $X of gains; the lots view shows which lots".
5. **Minimum-trade pass.** Repeat while some `sell | buy | use_cash` trade has `amount < minTradeCents`: take the smallest (sells before buys on a tie), remove it, `belowMinimumCents += amount`. A removed **buy** of class `c` in account `a`: `delta[c] += amount` (unmet) and the money stays in `a`'s cash: `delta.cash −= amount`, `a.hold_cash += amount` (created if absent). A removed **sell / use_cash** of class `c`: `delta[c] −= amount` (stays over target), then reduce `a`'s buys and `hold_cash` largest first by `amount`, reverting `delta[x.class] += reduced` (`delta.cash += reduced` for a reduced hold); a reduced trade may now itself be below the minimum and go in a later iteration. Finally net `use_cash` against `hold_cash` within each account (no delta change). `hold_cash` lines are never removed by this pass.
6. `residual` = classes with `delta ≠ 0` after the pass, with a note: "cannot be fully rebalanced without moving money between accounts" or, when the over-weight class is held only through blended funds, "<class> is held only through <fund>, which is held constant".
7. Output trades in the order generated (account rank, sells then buys); deep-equal for any input permutation.

### 3.13 Income by month and the feed (`incomeRows`, `incomeByMonth`; `services/investmentIncome.ts`, D14–D15)
- Rows in `(from, to]` by date: (a) `investment_txn` rows with `type ∈ dividend | interest` or (`type = reinvest` and `amount_cents > 0`) in the selected accounts; (b) ledger rows in investment accounts with `category_id = investment_income_category_id`, `is_transfer = false`, `pending = false`, `source ≠ 'investment'` and no `investment_txn` linked to them (`NOT EXISTS`). Each row → `{ id, date, month, accountId, securityId | null, kind, cents, reinvested, source: investment_txn | ledger, ledgerTransactionId, linkStatus ∈ linked | created | awaiting | not_fed | ledger_only, fed }`. `kind`: `interest` rows, and `dividend`/`reinvest` rows on `cash | cd | bond` securities, are `interest`; ledger rows are `interest` when the description matches `/INTEREST/i`, else `dividend`. `reinvested`: D14. `linkStatus`: `linked` when the link points at a bank row (`source ≠ investment`), `created` when it points at a synthetic row, else `awaiting` when the account's feed is `link | create`, else `not_fed`; `fed` = the linked row carries the feed category.
- Per month: `dividendsCents`, `interestCents`, `reinvestedCents` (subset of dividends), `totalCents = dividends + interest`, `bySecurity`, `byAccount`; period totals; `trailing12mCents` over `(addMonthsClamped(asOf, −12), asOf]`; `yieldBp = bp(trailing12m, classified total as of asOf)`. Rows are capped at 2 000 (`rowsTruncated`).
- The months this view reports equal the engine's `buildLots(...).income` for the same accounts and window (asserted in §7); the two readers differ only in that this one also unions ledger-only rows and carries per-row link state.

Feed run (`runInvestmentIncomeFeed(db, { accountIds? }) → { categorized, created, upgraded, awaiting, unchanged, deleted }`, one transaction per account, accounts with `investment_income_feed ≠ off`):
1. Load the household feed category; missing → the account is skipped with a logged warning (the PATCH that enables a feed refuses this state with `400 feed_category_required`).
2. Candidates = the account's feedable income rows (`dividend | interest`, not `reinvested`), by `(date, sequence, id)`.
3. For each candidate:
   a. Linked to a bank row → `UPDATE transaction SET category_id = feed, updated_at = now() WHERE id = link AND category_locked = false AND category_id IS DISTINCT FROM feed` → `categorized` when a row changed, else `unchanged`.
   b. Linked to a synthetic row (`source = 'investment'`) → **upgrade check**: the bank twin by the imports area's rule (same account, `amount_cents` equal, `|dayDiff| ≤ 3`, `is_transfer = false`, `pending = false`, `source ≠ 'investment'`, not linked to any `investment_txn`; nearest date, then earliest date, then id). Found → relink the candidate to the bank row, set the bank row's `category_id` / `category_locked` from the synthetic row (the owner's locked choice travels; otherwise the feed category, unlocked), delete the synthetic row → `upgraded`. Not found → ensure the synthetic row's category is the feed category unless locked → `unchanged`.
   c. Unlinked: mode `create` → insert `{ account_id, source 'investment', external_id 'inv:' + id, date, amount_cents, raw_description = type upper + ' ' + (ticker ?? name ?? ''), category_id feed, category_locked false, pending false, is_transfer false }` (idempotent through the existing unique `(account_id, external_id)`), set the link → `created`. Mode `link` → `awaiting`.
4. Mode change to `off` (`setInvestmentIncomeFeed`): delete the account's synthetic rows with `category_locked = false` (`deleted`; the links clear by `ON DELETE SET NULL`); locked synthetic rows stay as the owner's own rows.
Idempotent: a second run reports everything `unchanged`. Each account is its own transaction so one bad account never rolls back the others.
Worked (seed; B's seven income rows are already linked by the imports area, the VTI dividend of 09-28 to the bank row of 09-29): B at `link` → run → `categorized 7`; plan income "Dividends" for 2026-09 = bank rows 09-22 4 150 + 09-29 4 910 + 09-30 410 = **9 470** (three rows); second run `unchanged 7`. R switched to `create` → `created 4` (2 240, 2 260, 2 280, 2 455; `external_id` `inv:<id>`); plan 2026-09 = 9 470 + 2 260 + 2 455 = **14 185** (five rows). A bank row +2 280 dated 2026-10-07 "DIVIDEND BND" appearing in R → next run `upgraded 1`: the 10-06 synthetic row is gone, the bank row is linked and categorized. R back to `off` → `deleted 3`; the bank row keeps its category and link.

**Plan loader rule (one clause).** `loadActuals`, `loadUncategorized` and `countUncategorized` (the imports area adds `account.on_budget = true` to all three) read `… WHERE (account.on_budget = true OR transaction.category_id = household_setting.investment_income_category_id)`; uncategorized counters are unaffected because feed rows are categorized by definition. Nothing else in Phase 2 changes.

**Exclusions.** `pairTransfersInDb` and `matchOne` (recurring) add `transaction.source <> 'investment' AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM investment_txn i WHERE i.ledger_transaction_id = transaction.id AND i.type IN ('dividend','interest','reinvest','fee'))` to their candidate queries; cash-transfer links (`type = 'transfer'`) are *not* excluded (those ledger rows are transfers and must pair).

Worker job `investments.income_feed` (`apps/worker/src/jobs/investmentIncomeFeed.ts`)
- Payload `{ reason: 'sync' | 'import' | 'setting' | 'nightly' | 'manual', accountId?, day? }`; `dedupe: true` (the existing `enqueueJob` dedupes by type when the payload has no `connectionId`: one queued or running at a time; the next enqueue after completion catches anything new).
- Triggers: `runSyncJob` after a successful full sync (one line next to the `recurring.detect` enqueue; the imports area's `linkUnlinkedInvestmentTxns` has run inside that sync, so new bank twins are linked before the feed categorizes them); the trade-history import commit (imports area, one line); `setInvestmentIncomeFeed`; the daily tick (`DAILY_JOB_TYPES` gains it after `recurring.detect`, with the same `{ reason: 'nightly', day }` guard); `POST /investments/income/feed/run` runs inline and does not enqueue.
- Handler: `runInvestmentIncomeFeed(ctx.db, payload.accountId ? { accountIds: [payload.accountId] } : {})`; returns the counts for the job log.
- Idempotent (links are unique, synthetic rows are unique by `(account_id, external_id)`); failure: a thrown error marks the job failed with the runner's backoff (`failJob`); per-account work is its own transaction; an account without a feed category is skipped with a logged warning.

Dependencies on other areas' jobs (none block this area; views degrade with staleness flags): `prices.fetch` (prices area, nightly after market close, held securities only) and `investments.valuation` (prices area: writes the `computed` snapshots of D3 after every price fetch and positions import). No other jobs: the views are request-time reads.

## 8. Deviations from the spec (flagged)

1. **OFX/QFX import** is marked phase 3 in the spec's data-sources table but Polish in its build-phase list; treated as out of scope here (the build list is the schedule). Every view reads the stored tables, so an OFX adapter that writes `holding_snapshot` / `investment_txn` through the imports area's services needs nothing from this area.
2. **Rounding**: the engine's half-away-from-zero conversions (R1–R3) are used here as they are, next to Phase 2's truncation for every ratio this area derives (D2). Both rules are stated once.
3. **Cash as a security and as a residual**: the spec's `security` row lists type `cash`; using it for reported cash positions follows the spec; treating the positive gap between the account balance and the positions as "Cash (unlisted)" is an interpretation (the spec does not say where uninvested cash goes). A negative gap is a warning, never negative cash.
4. **`security_allocation`** (blended-fund splits), **`account_reconciliation`**, the three `household_setting` columns, `account.investment_income_feed`, `transaction.source = 'investment'` and the partial unique index on the ledger link are additions beyond the spec's rows; the spec describes targets, "feeding the income bucket if desired" and the gate as behaviour, not tables.
5. **Fidelity is derived, not stored**; the spec says each account "shows which one it has", which a derived value satisfies and can never go stale. The engine area's stored `account.investment_history` is a conflict for the Phase 3 plan (§9).
6. **Allocation is over positions-level accounts only**; balance-only accounts are shown as unclassified rather than forced into "other".
7. **Benchmark is price-return only** until the prices area supplies adjusted closes; the view labels it (`priceOnly`).
8. **Returns are the engine's floats on the wire**; weights, drift and gain percentages are integer basis points. Design A's "every rate as integer bp" was dropped so the five pages and the engine's own routes agree on one representation.
9. **Rebalance** prefers tax-advantaged accounts by a fixed rank (`pre_tax = hsa = roth < none < taxable`), never moves money across accounts, holds blended funds constant and estimates gains pro rata on average unrealized gain rather than by FIFO lot; the spec only says "prefers tax-advantaged accounts for trades". The lots view shows the exact lots.
10. **Reinvested dividends are income but are never fed to the plan** (no cash arrived); the spec's "feeding the income bucket" applies to cash dividends and interest.
11. **Phase 2 code gains three one-clause changes**: the plan's actual loaders read rows in the feed category from off-budget accounts; transfer pairing and recurring matching skip feed rows and rows linked to income `investment_txn` rows. Without the first, the imports area's `on_budget` filter (its Deviation 12) would hide every fed dividend; without the second, a dividend could pair with an unrelated outflow or be reported as a new subscription.
12. **Staleness thresholds** (7 days for prices and valuations, 35 days for positions) are constants, not settings.

## 10. Open questions

5. **Benchmark total return**: accept price-only with the label, or require the prices area to store adjusted closes from the chosen provider?
6. **Turning a `create` feed off**: delete synthetic rows (this design, unlocked rows only) or keep them as manual rows?

8. **Dollar-only positions and allocation**: a dollar-only row's class comes from its security; should the Allocation page flag that the quantity is unknown (currently only the Holdings page says "value only")?
