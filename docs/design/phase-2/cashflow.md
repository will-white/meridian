## summary

Cash-flow projection is one pure, deterministic function `projectCashFlow(input)` in packages/shared/src/cashflow.ts (BigInt cents, YYYY-MM-DD strings, no clock) that rolls every projected on-budget open account forward from `today` through `today + horizonDays` inclusive (horizonDays + 1 points; 90 days = 91 points; a 12-month horizon is dayDiff(today, addMonths(today, 12)) days, max 366). Per account: opening balance (posted balance + pending transactions) + recurring income on due dates − bills on due dates ± recurring transfer legs (each leg posts only when its account is projected, so on-budget transfers cancel in the combined line) − credit-card payments − the month's remaining planned spending (spending bucket only) spread over the remaining days of the month. Card payments are modeled as "pay the card's balance as of the statement close on `account.payment_day` from `account.payment_from_account_id`" (statement close = `account.statement_day`, or the day before the payment when null), not as a detected recurring transfer: the amount then follows what the simulation put on the card and what-ifs flow through to checking; a detected transfer into a card that has a payment day is excluded by the loader so nothing is counted twice. Discretionary spending is split across accounts by largest remainder on each account's trailing-90-day spending-bucket outflows (fallback: household primary account, then the first checking account), then spread with base = floor(S/N) and the first (S mod N) days carrying one extra cent, so every split and spread sums exactly. Thresholds: per-account override, else the household default for checking/savings only (credit/loan/investment never inherit); crossing is strict (<) on end-of-day balances, with `alreadyBelow` for an opening below the line; the combined line uses the household default. What-ifs (skipOccurrence, moveOccurrence one/all by shiftDays, oneTime amount) are request-body inputs, applied to original occurrence dates in body order, never persisted, each reported back as applied or not with a reason. Schema: columns only on `recurring` (account_id NOT NULL, transfer_account_id, day_of_month_2, signed expected_amount_cents) and `account` (low_balance_threshold_cents, payment_day, payment_from_account_id, statement_day), plus a single-row `household_setting` table (default threshold, primary account, default horizon, timezone) that other Phase 2 areas reuse. One endpoint, `POST /api/cashflow/projection`, runs the engine inline (sub-10 ms; no worker job, no writes). ## dataModel

### No new projection tables. The result is recomputed per request (≤ 367 points × a handful of accounts); what-ifs, results and weights are never stored.

### recurring (Phase 2 table from the spec; columns this area depends on — the recurring area owns the rest)
- `account_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES account(id) ON DELETE CASCADE` — the account the money leaves (bill, transfer) or enters (income). Required: an item without an account cannot be placed on a line. Detection groups by (normalized payee, account) and uses the group's account; manual items pick one (UI defaults to the household primary).
- `transfer_account_id uuid NULL REFERENCES account(id) ON DELETE SET NULL` — destination for items detected from paired transfer legs or created by hand (checking → savings, checking → brokerage, autopay into an on-budget loan). Null for bills and income. Kind is derived, not stored: transfer_account_id set → `transfer`; else amount ≥ 0 → `income`; else `bill`. Items whose category is in the spending bucket are not dated events (the spread already covers them). Justification: without it, savings contributions and loan autopays would need a second mechanism, and they must leave the budget as transfers, not bills.
- `day_of_month_2 smallint NULL CHECK (day_of_month_2 BETWEEN 1 AND 31)` — second day for cadence `twice_monthly` (first day = day of `next_due_date`; 31 means last day of month). Null → derived from the anchor day D: D ≤ 15 → {D, D+15}, else {D−15, D} (15th & 30th, 1st & 16th, 16th & 31st→clamped). Detection sets it from the observed pair when the two days are not 15 apart.
- `expected_amount_cents bigint NOT NULL` — **signed like `transaction.amount_cents`** (income positive, bills negative). `tolerance_cents` is irrelevant here.
- `cadence text NOT NULL CHECK IN ('weekly','biweekly','twice_monthly','monthly','quarterly','semiannual','yearly')`, `next_due_date date NOT NULL`, `status text NOT NULL CHECK IN ('suggested','active','paused','cancelled')`. Only `active` is projected.
- Indexes: `recurring_account_status_idx (account_id, status)`, `recurring_status_due_idx (status, next_due_date)` (also serves the "bills due in 14 days" dashboard card).

### account (packages/db/src/schema/accounts.ts) — four nullable columns
- `low_balance_threshold_cents bigint NULL` — per-account override. Null → checking/savings inherit `household_setting.low_balance_threshold_cents`; credit, loan, brokerage and the retirement types get no threshold unless set explicitly (a negative card balance is normal, not "low").
- `payment_day smallint NULL CHECK (1..31)` — credit accounts only; the day each month the card is paid, clamped to the month's length (31 = last day). Non-null is the switch that turns card-payment projection on.
- `payment_from_account_id uuid NULL REFERENCES account(id) ON DELETE SET NULL` — the paying account; must be an on-budget checking or savings account and ≠ the card. `CHECK ((payment_day IS NULL) = (payment_from_account_id IS NULL))`.
- `statement_day smallint NULL CHECK (1..31)` — statement close day. Null = "pay the balance as of the end of the day before the payment date" (simplest, conservative by at most one cycle's post-statement spending). Set = "pay the balance as of the most recent close date strictly before the payment date" (what the bank does).
- App-level validation in PATCH /api/accounts/:id: the three payment columns are rejected (400) on non-credit accounts. Columns on `account` were chosen over a separate `card_payment_setting` table: the switch is `payment_day IS NOT NULL`, the constraints are expressible as CHECKs, and there is no fixed-amount mode (a household that pays a fixed amount creates a recurring transfer item and leaves `payment_day` null).

### household_setting (new, single row, `id smallint PRIMARY KEY CHECK (id = 1)`, created on first read)
- `low_balance_threshold_cents bigint NOT NULL DEFAULT 0` — default for cash accounts and for the combined line (0 = "goes negative").
- `primary_account_id uuid NULL REFERENCES account(id) ON DELETE SET NULL` — carries discretionary spending when no spending history exists; default account for manual recurring items.
- `projection_default_days integer NOT NULL DEFAULT 90 CHECK (BETWEEN 1 AND 366)`.
- `timezone text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'America/Denver'` — the API computes the household's "today" from it (UTC "today" is wrong after 5 pm Mountain).
- `updated_at timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT now()`.
Why a table and not a request input remembered in localStorage: the threshold and primary account are household facts, must agree across devices and areas (dashboard card, zero-based toggle, grid defaults can add columns here), and the primary account needs a single owner (one nullable FK beats a partial unique index on `account.is_primary`).

### Loader reads (packages/db/src/services/cashflow.ts, `loadCashFlowInput`)
1. Projected accounts: `on_budget AND NOT closed` [AND id IN accountIds], ordered by name then id. `openingCents = COALESCE(balance_cents, 0) + Σ amount_cents of transactions WHERE pending AND account_id = a` (documented assumption, one place: SimpleFIN `balance` is the posted balance and excludes pending; `available_cents` is not used because for cards it means remaining credit). Null balance → warning `account X has no known balance`. `balance_date` passed through.
2. Recurring: `status = 'active'`; derived kind; spending-bucket items excluded; transfer items whose `transfer_account_id` is a credit account with `payment_day IS NOT NULL` excluded (no double counting).
3. Card payments: for each projected credit account with `payment_day`: `{cardAccountId, fromAccountId, payDay, statementDay, knownStatementBalanceCents}` where `knownStatementBalanceCents = openingCents − Σ amount_cents of the card's transactions (posted and pending) WITH S < date ≤ today`, S = the latest statement close date strictly before the first in-horizon payment date (only computed when `statement_day` is set and S < today; else null).
4. Spending plan per month from today's month through the horizon's last month, spending bucket only: current month `remainingCents = Σ over spending-bucket categories of max(0, planned + carried_in − actual_to_date)` where actual_to_date = −Σ amount_cents of non-transfer transactions (posted or pending, bucket via COALESCE(bucket_override, category.bucket)) dated ≤ today in that category; future months `remainingCents = Σ resolved planned` from the plan area's month resolver (manual > recurring > average > none), so an unplanned month 6 carries the average, not 0. Planned savings contributions and bills-bucket plans are never spread: they enter only as dated recurring items.
5. Spending weights: per projected account, `Σ −amount_cents` of non-transfer outflows in spending-bucket categories dated in [today − 89, today] (90 days inclusive; never empty on the 1st, dampens a thin month-to-date sample). Ordered by account name then id (tie order for largest remainder).
6. `primaryAccountId` and `defaultThresholdCents` from `household_setting` (request `thresholdCents` overrides the latter when present).

- `packages/shared/src/dates.ts` gains `daysInMonth(year, month1to12)`, `monthOf(date)` ("YYYY-MM"), `firstOfMonth`, `lastOfMonth`, `addMonthsClamped(anchor, months, dayOfMonth)`.

### Steps
1. **Window.** `endDate = addDays(today, horizonDays)`; `dates[i] = addDays(today, i)` for i = 0..horizonDays. `delta[a][i] = 0n` for every projected account; `has(id)` = account is in `input.accounts`; `idx(d) = dayDiff(today, d)`.
2. **Occurrences** (`occurrencesBetween`, anchor = `nextDueDate`, ad = its day of month). `maxShift = max(0, |shiftDays| over moveOccurrence what-ifs)`; generate through `addDays(endDate, maxShift)` so a backward "all" shift can pull the next occurrence into the window and a forward one can push one out.
   - weekly / biweekly: anchor + 7k / 14k, k ≥ 0.
   - monthly / quarterly / semiannual / yearly (step 1/3/6/12): occurrence k = `addMonthsClamped(anchor, step·k, ad)` — always from the anchor with the anchor's day, clamped to the target month, never iterated from the previous occurrence: Oct 31 → Nov 30 → Dec 31 → Jan 31 → Feb 28 → Mar 31; quarterly from Nov 30 → Feb 28 (not Mar 2).
   - twice_monthly: days {ad, d2} with d2 = `dayOfMonth2 ?? (ad ≤ 15 ? ad + 15 : ad − 15)`, each clamped per month (31 = last day), sorted, dropping dates before the anchor.
   - **Overdue collapse:** occurrences dated before `today` are reduced to the single latest one, kept with its `originalDate` and `overdue = true` (an unpaid bill or late paycheck still lands once; a three-month-stale item does not post three times — the missed-charge alert is where the user fixes it).
3. **What-ifs on occurrences** (matched on `originalDate`, applied in body order, each recorded in `result.whatIfs[index]` as applied or not with a reason: `unknown recurring id`, `no occurrence of <id> on <date>`). `skipOccurrence` with `occurrenceDate === originalDate` → event kept with `applied = false`, nothing posted; skip beats move. `moveOccurrence` applies when `occurrenceDate` is absent or equals `originalDate`; shifts add up when several match; `date = addDays(originalDate, Σshift)`, `moved = true`. Then `date < today` → `date = today, overdue = true`; `date > endDate` → dropped.
4. **Post the event.** Primary leg: if `has(accountId)`: event (kind income/bill/transfer_out) and, when applied, `delta[accountId][idx(date)] += amountCents`. Transfer second leg: if `transferAccountId && has(transferAccountId)`: event kind transfer_in with `−amountCents` and `delta[transferAccountId][idx] −= amountCents`. A leg whose account is not projected is silently absent (a transfer to an off-budget brokerage lowers the combined line; one to on-budget savings leaves it unchanged).
5. **oneTime what-ifs:** `!has(accountId)` → not applied, reason `account not projected`; `date > endDate` → not applied, `after horizon`; `date < today` → posts on today, applied with reason `moved to today`; else `delta[accountId][idx(date)] += amountCents` and a `one_time` event.
6. **Spending spread** (spending bucket only). For each `spendingPlan` month whose [first, last] intersects [today, endDate]: `A = max(0n, remainingCents)`; `windowStart = max(first, today)`; `N = dayDiff(windowStart, last) + 1` (current month: days left including today; future months: full month length, **even when the horizon ends mid-month** — only in-window days are posted, the daily amount is never recomputed over the shorter window). `shares = splitByWeights(A, weights ∩ projected)`; null → `{primary: A}` where primary = `primaryAccountId` if projected, else the first projected `checking` by name, else the first projected account; none → warning, nothing posted. For each share `S_a`: `base = S_a / N` (BigInt division on non-negatives = floor), `rem = S_a − base·N` (0 ≤ rem < N); day i (0-based from windowStart) gets `base + (i < rem ? 1n : 0n)`, posted as `delta[a][idx] −= amount` for days ≤ endDate; Σ over the full window = `base·N + rem = S_a` exactly; extra cents go to the earliest days (deterministic, slightly conservative). One `CashFlowSpread` record per (month, account). No per-day spending events.
   `splitByWeights(A, w)`: `W = Σ w_i`; `W = 0n` → null; `share_i = (A·w_i) / W`, `r_i = (A·w_i) mod W`; `left = A − Σ share_i` (0 ≤ left < n) goes +1 to the `left` accounts with the largest `r_i`, ties by input order.
7. **Day loop** i = 0..horizonDays, date d. First card payments: for each `cardPayments` entry with card and from-account projected and `day(d) === min(payDay, daysInMonth(d))`: statement date `S = statementDay === null ? addDays(d, −1) : clamp(statementDay) in d's month, or in the previous month if that date ≥ d`. Statement balance `B = S ≥ today ? points[card][idx(S)] : (knownStatementBalanceCents ?? openingCents(card))`. `owed = max(0n, −B)`; if `owed > 0n`: `delta[from][i] −= owed`, `delta[card][i] += owed`, events `card_payment_out` (from) and `card_payment_in` (card). Since S < d always, only earlier indexes are read: the single forward pass is well defined. Then `points[a][i] = (i === 0 ? openingCents : points[a][i−1]) + delta[a][i]` for every account. The combined line is unchanged by a payment by construction. A projected credit account with no payment entry yields warning `card <name> has no payment day`; a payment whose paying account is not projected yields a warning and no payment.
8. **Series stats.** `combined.points[i] = Σ_a points[a][i]`, `combined.openingCents = Σ opening`. Per account `thresholdCents = lowBalanceThresholdCents ?? (type ∈ {checking, savings} ? defaultThresholdCents : null)`; combined uses `defaultThresholdCents`. `minCents/minDate` = minimum end-of-day balance, earliest date on ties. `firstBelowThresholdDate` = first `dates[i]` with `points[i] < threshold` (strict; equal is not below), null when never crossed or threshold null; may be `today`. `alreadyBelow = threshold !== null && openingCents < threshold`.
9. **Determinism.** Recurring items processed in `id` order; what-ifs in body order; events sorted by (date, accountId, kind, recurringId ?? '', originalDate ?? ''); accounts returned in input order; weight ties by input order. Identical input ⇒ deep-equal output; the route performs no writes.

### Integer-math rules (the only divisions in the engine)
`S_a / N` and `(A·w_i) / W` on dividends clamped ≥ 0 and divisors ≥ 1, so BigInt truncation equals floor and `%` is never negative; remainders are redistributed one cent at a time (earliest days for the daily spread, largest remainder for the account split); every spread and split sums exactly to its input. Negative "remaining" (overspent) is treated as 0 before any division. No averages, no rounding anywhere else; the UI formats with `formatCents`. The dashboard's "daily pace" (plan area) must call `spreadEvenly(remaining, daysLeftInMonth)` so both numbers agree.

## edgeCases

- Horizon is inclusive of both ends: horizonDays = 90 gives 91 points (today … today+90); an item due on endDate is inside, one due on endDate+1 is outside; a 12-month horizon is dayDiff(today, addMonths(today, 12)) = 365 or 366 days, computed by the client.
- Monthly-family cadences step from the anchor with the anchor's day and clamp to the target month: Oct 31 → Nov 30 → Dec 31 → Jan 31 → Feb 28 (29 in leap years) → Mar 31; iterating from the previous occurrence would drift to the 28th forever, and 'quarterly = +90 days' lands on the wrong day. Quarterly from 2026-11-30 falls on 2027-02-28, not 2027-03-02 (JS Date overflow).
- twice_monthly: day_of_month_2 null derives the pair from the anchor day (D ≤ 15 → {D, D+15}; else {D−15, D}); 31 means last day; both days are clamped per month and dates before the anchor are dropped (anchor 2026-10-20 with second day 5 starts 10-20, then 11-05).
- Overdue items (next_due_date < today) are collapsed to one occurrence posted on today, flagged overdue with the original date; the regular schedule continues from the anchor. A 3-month-stale item still posts once. If the charge is actually already in the balance (matcher rejected it), the missed-charge alert is where the user fixes it; the projection does not guess.
- Items due today are included; the recurring matcher advances next_due_date when it matches today's charge, so an already-posted charge is not counted twice as long as matching runs after the sync (same job).
- Opening balance = posted balance + Σ pending transaction amounts; pending spending is also inside 'actual to date', so a pending grocery charge reduces the spread and the opening consistently. available_cents is never used (for cards it means remaining credit). Null balance → opening 0 plus pending, with a warning.
- A stale balance_date (sync broken for days) is passed through, not corrected; the UI shows 'as of'.
- Spending remaining is clamped at 0 per category before summing (an overspent category does not create room elsewhere) and again in the engine; the engine never divides a negative number.
- Daily spread: base = floor(A/N), the first (A mod N) days get one extra cent; Σ = A exactly for 28, 29, 30, 31 and 19-day windows; the spreads record exposes base and extraCentDays so tests assert the distribution, not just the total.
- Horizon ends mid-month: the last month's per-day amount still uses the full month length; only in-window days are posted (31,000 over November with 2 in-window days posts 1,034 + 1,034, not 31,000).
- Today = last day of the month: N = 1, the whole remaining amount posts today; the next month starts its normal spread tomorrow.
- Account split by largest remainder: weights 20,000 : 10,000 on 25,000 give 16,667 + 8,333 (not 16,666 + 8,333); ties go to the earlier account in input order (name, then id).
- All spending weights zero (new install, 90 quiet days, or every spending account closed): everything goes to the primary account; no primary and no checking: first projected account; no accounts at all: warning, nothing spread.
- Transfers: each leg posts only if its account is projected; checking → on-budget savings leaves the combined line unchanged, checking → off-budget brokerage lowers it, brokerage → checking raises it; a subset projection (accountIds) therefore changes the combined line by design and the response lists the included accounts.
- Card payment with statement_day set and the close date before today reads the ledger-derived balance at the close (knownStatementBalanceCents); charges after the close are paid the following month. statement_day null pays the balance as of the end of the day before the payment date (post-statement charges are paid one cycle early: conservative for the paying account).
- Card in credit (balance ≥ 0) on payment day: owed = 0, no payment event. Payment day 31 in February clamps to the 28th/29th; statement_day == payment_day (or later in the month) resolves to the previous month's close.
- A projected credit account with no payment day accumulates spending on its own line and never hits checking; the response carries a warning naming the card so the UI can prompt for settings. A card whose paying account is not projected (off-budget or filtered out) is not paid and warns.
- A recurring transfer into a card that has a payment day is excluded by the loader so the payment is not counted twice; the UI blocks creating both.
- Threshold crossing is strict: a balance equal to the threshold is not below (50,000 on Oct 22 with threshold 50,000 is not a crossing; 49,000 on Oct 23 is). alreadyBelow is computed from the opening balance so a day-0 crossing is distinguishable from 'crossed later'; firstBelowThresholdDate may equal today.
- Credit, loan, brokerage and retirement accounts never inherit the household threshold (a card at −45,000 with a 50,000 default must not report a crossing); an explicit per-account override still applies.
- What-ifs match on ORIGINAL occurrence dates, so body order never changes which occurrence they hit; skip beats move on the same occurrence; several moves on one occurrence add up; a move that lands before today clamps to today (overdue = true); a move that lands after endDate removes the occurrence; a backward 'all' shift pulls the next occurrence from beyond the horizon into it (7 paychecks become 8), which is why generation runs through endDate + maxShift.
- A what-if naming a non-existent occurrence date or an unknown recurring id is reported (applied = false, reason) and leaves balances untouched; it is not an HTTP error.
- oneTime before today posts today (applied, reason 'moved to today'); after endDate or on a non-projected account is reported as not applied; a positive amount models a windfall. Duplicate what-ifs are applied as given (two identical oneTime items post twice; two identical skips are one skip).
- Recurring items in spending-bucket categories are not dated events (the spread covers them); items in the savings or bills bucket without a transfer account are dated outflows (401k ACH to an external account).
- Items with status suggested/paused/cancelled are excluded; pausing an item is the persistent way to 'skip' it (what-ifs are per request).
- Determinism: shuffling the recurring, accounts, cardPayments or spendingWeights arrays does not change any series or the event order; running twice returns deep-equal output; the route performs no writes.
- Timezone: 'today' comes from household_setting.timezone, not UTC; a client-supplied today is accepted only within ±1 day to allow for the browser's clock.
- Validation: horizonDays outside 1..366, shiftDays outside −366..366 or 0, more than 50 what-ifs, or a non-integer cents string are rejected by Zod with 400.
- Weekend/holiday shifting of bank postings, interest accrual and amount tolerance are not modeled; the expected amount is used as-is (documented).

## risks

- Opening-balance assumption: SimpleFIN `balance` is treated as the posted balance, so the loader adds pending amounts. A bank that already nets pending into `balance` double counts every pending charge for a day or two. The rule lives in one loader function; import_log makes it checkable per institution and a per-connection toggle can be added if real data shows it.
- Manual bills-bucket plans with no recurring item (a value typed into the grid) have no due date and are not forecast; the spread covers only the spending bucket. The UI should show 'N planned bills without a schedule are not forecast' and offer to create a recurring item. Spreading them instead would double count the recurring auto-fill.
- Planned savings-goal contributions without a recurring transfer item are likewise not forecast; same remedy (create the transfer item). Automatic transfers detected from paired legs cover the common case.
- Credit cards without a payment day make checking look better than it is; until set, the response carries a warning naming the card. The recurring detector should propose payment_day (median day of the paired checking→card legs) as a suggestion.
- Overdue items rolled to today double count when the real charge posted but the matcher rejected it (amount outside tolerance); the missed-charge alert surfaces exactly that case; the projection intentionally does not guess.
- Cross-area dependency: recurring detection must group by (payee, account) and persist account_id, transfer_account_id and day_of_month_2, and expected_amount_cents must be signed; the loader is the one place to adapt if the recurring area stores an absolute amount plus direction, but the engine tests assume signed input.
- Cross-area dependency: the current month's remaining spending must come from the plan area's per-category remaining (planned + carried_in − actual, clamped at 0 per category), and future months from its resolver with the average fallback; the formulas are pinned here so the two areas agree. If the resolver returns 0 for unplanned months, 12-month projections become optimistic.
- Trailing-90-day spending weights lag a real change (a new card used for all groceries takes weeks to dominate); acceptable for a household and tunable in one loader constant.
- Statement-day mode depends on a correct knownStatementBalanceCents from the ledger; a card with a stale sync falls back to the opening balance and overpays by the post-close charges once; the balance_date in the response is the user's cue.
- Timezone: ledger dates are UTC calendar dates from SimpleFIN while 'today' comes from household_setting.timezone; near midnight an item can move between 'overdue today' and 'due tomorrow' by one day. Acceptable for a forecast; documented.
- A 'move all' what-if extends occurrence generation by |shift| beyond the horizon; shiftDays is capped at ±366 so the extension is bounded; horizonDays ≤ 366 with ~15 accounts yields ~5,500 points per response (under 200 KB JSON), fine inline. If the household ever has dozens of accounts, paginate the response per account rather than moving the engine to the worker.
- No persisted projection means no 'forecast vs actual' history; the spec does not ask for it and recomputation is cheap, but a future report would need snapshots.
- Weekend/holiday posting shifts, variable-amount bills and interest accrual are not modeled; the recurring area's expected amount (median) is the right central estimate but will be off in any given month.

## resolutions

- Card payment model: both designs chose 'pay the card's balance on day N from a paying account' over a detected recurring transfer, for the same reason (the amount follows what the simulation put on the card and what-ifs flow through to checking); adopted. Storage: B's columns on `account` (payment_day, payment_from_account_id) rather than A's `card_payment_setting` table, because `payment_day IS NOT NULL` is the switch and the constraints are plain CHECKs; A's optional `statement_day` is kept because it is one nullable column and makes the paying account's line match the bank. A's fixed-amount mode is dropped: a fixed payer creates a recurring transfer item and leaves payment_day null. A's loader rule that excludes a detected transfer into a card with a payment day is kept to prevent double counting.
- Payment amount basis: A pays the balance as of the end of the day BEFORE the payment date (statement_day null) while B pays the balance after the payment day's own flows. Decided for A, unified as 'balance at the end of statement date S, S = statement close or payment date − 1': a bank autopay amount is fixed from the statement, not from same-day charges, and it keeps the single forward pass free of same-day circularity. B's case 5 was recomputed under this rule (payment 25,530 instead of 26,320; checking ends at 114,470, card at −9,471; combined 104,999 unchanged) and is test 20.
- Payment day range: A's 1..31 clamped to the month length over B's 1..28, so a card due on the 30th/31st can be entered as-is; February clamping is tested (test 23).
- Household settings: A's single-row `household_setting` table (default threshold, primary account, default horizon, timezone) over B's request-input threshold remembered in localStorage and `account.is_primary`. Reason: the threshold and primary account are household facts that must agree across devices and areas (dashboard card, zero-based toggle), and one nullable FK is simpler than a partial unique index. B's request-level `thresholdCents` is kept as an optional per-request override for the slider.
- Spending weights: A's trailing 90 days (today − 89 … today) over B's month-to-date with previous-month fallback. B itself lists the thin-sample swing as a risk and names trailing days as the fix; 90 days is never empty on the 1st.
- Current-month remaining: A's per-category clamp (Σ max(0, planned + carried_in − actual)) over B's bucket-level clamp, so an overspent category cannot offset others. Future months: B's plan resolver (manual > recurring > average > none) over A's 'no plan rows contribute 0', because the lead's decision tracks the planned value's source and a 12-month projection with zeros after month 2 would be useless.
- Overdue items: B's single collapsed occurrence (the latest missed one, posted today, flagged overdue) over A's 'every missed occurrence rolls to today', which would post a 3-month-stale item three times today. Test 15(b) covers it.
- twice_monthly: A's nullable `day_of_month_2` column is kept (1st & 16th vs 16th & 31st cannot be derived from one anchor), with B's derivation ({D, D+15} or {D−15, D}) applied when it is null so manual items need no second field. Cadence name is `twice_monthly` (lead's wording).
- What-if shape: merged. skipOccurrence {recurringId, occurrenceDate}; moveOccurrence {recurringId, shiftDays, occurrenceDate?} (B's 'absent = all' instead of A's scope enum; A's `newDate` dropped because the client can compute shiftDays = dayDiff); oneTime {date, accountId, amountCents, label?}. A's ±366 shift range and 'generate through endDate + maxShift' rule are kept (test 10 needs them; B's ±31 cannot move a payday across a month boundary twice). A's per-what-if applied/reason reporting is kept over B's silent no-op because the UI needs to show a stale what-if after a due date advances. Both agree what-ifs match original dates and skip beats move.
- Events: A's typed kinds (income, bill, transfer_out, transfer_in, card_payment_out, card_payment_in, one_time) with applied/overdue/moved flags, and no per-day spending events (B's 'spending' kind would add 90 rows per account); the spreads array is the audit record instead.
- Endpoint: one POST /api/cashflow/projection (B); A's GET variant dropped since the dashboard sends { horizonDays: 30 } to the same route. Both agree it runs inline with no worker job and no writes.
- Result field names: A's (openingCents, points, minCents/minDate, thresholdCents, firstBelowThresholdDate, alreadyBelow) over B's (startCents, lowPoint, firstBelow); alreadyBelow is kept because a day-0 crossing must be distinguishable from a later one.
- 'today': A's household timezone default plus a client override accepted within ±1 day; B's server-UTC default is wrong after 5 pm Mountain.
- Horizon: both agree on horizonDays + 1 inclusive points (90 days = 91); kept.
- Recurring indexes: both of A's (account_id, status) and B's (status, next_due_date) are adopted; they are cheap and serve different queries.
- Test corrections: A's test 11 mixed numbers from a one-what-if run and a four-what-if run; rewritten with the four-what-if numbers only (10-13 = 249,000; 11-19 = 699,000; end 1,119,000). A's test 9(a) used newDate 2026-10-20, re-expressed as shiftDays 4 with identical results. B's helper cases (spreadEvenly 30,000/19; splitByWeights 25,001) were re-verified and folded into test 24. B's case 12 was recomputed under the merged rules (identical numbers) and is test 21. Every other number was re-derived with the reference run; none were wrong.
- Spec coverage check: every sentence of the spec's cash-flow paragraph (per-account 90 days adjustable to 12 months; income, bills, card payments, remaining planned spending spread; per-account and combined lines; threshold and first crossing; skip a bill, one-time expense, move a payday; dashboard 30-day line with the low point) is covered by both designs and by the merge; nothing required by the spec was missing. Added beyond both: the overdue-collapse rule, the paying-account-not-projected warning, the last-day-of-month and February clamping tests, and the pinned formulas for the two cross-area dependencies (plan 'remaining' and the recurring columns).
