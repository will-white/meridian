# Phase 3 build plan: Investments

Source of truth: the design spec linked from `CLAUDE.md`. Four area designs were produced by independent designers, scored by judges and merged; a seam critic then read all four against the spec and the code. They live in `docs/design/phase-3/`:

- `imports-and-data.md`: tables, security master, SimpleFIN `extra` holdings, positions and trade CSV importers, manual entry, contributions, fidelity.
- `lots-and-performance.md`: the lot engine (FIFO, specific lot, splits, transfers), realized and unrealized gains, income by month, XIRR, true TWR and Modified Dietz, benchmark.
- `prices-and-valuation.md`: the price provider (Tiingo), the fetch job, implied prices, the valuation chain, value series, day and period change, reconciliation to a statement.
- `views-and-api.md`: Portfolio, Allocation (targets, drift, rebalance), Holdings, Performance, Income views; the income feed into the plan; dashboard additions; the web pages.
- `seams.md`: the critic's reconciled decisions (§1), every conflict with its resolution (§2), spec gaps (§3), Phase 1/2 contradictions (§4), the build order (§5).

Precedence: the spec, then **this document**, then `seams.md`, then the area design. Where an area design and `seams.md` disagree, `seams.md` wins; where this document and `seams.md` disagree, this document wins. An implementer builds from the area design for the algorithms, contracts and test cases, with the names and shapes below.

Scope (spec, Phase 3): securities, daily prices, holdings from SimpleFIN `extra` and positions CSV, trade-history import, lots and cost basis, allocation and performance views. Gate: account values match brokerage statements to the dollar on a chosen date. OFX/QFX import is **not** Phase 3 (the spec's data-sources table says phase 3, its build-phase list says Polish; the build list wins and the conflict is flagged); the importers consume `MappedPosition[]` / `MappedTrade[]` so an OFX adapter is a parser only.

## Reconciled decisions (binding)

Conventions carried from Phases 1 and 2: money is `bigint` cents; dates `YYYY-MM-DD`; months `YYYY-MM`; cents travel as decimal strings; `today` comes from the client within ±2 days of the household date (`resolveToday`); one audit batch per request and never when before equals after; typed service errors (`PlanError(status, code, message)`) mapped to HTTP by the routes; `not_found` with the entity named in the message for every 404.

1. **Numbers.** Share quantities and prices are `numeric(20,8)` in Postgres and **Fixed8** in TypeScript: `bigint` scaled by 10⁸, decimal strings on the wire (`Fixed8String = /^-?\d{1,12}(\.\d{1,8})?$/`, `NonNegFixed8String`; aliases `QuantityString`, `PriceString`). One module `packages/shared/src/decimal.ts` exports `parseFixed8`, `formatFixed8` (canonical, no trailing zeros), `roundDivHalfAway`, `mulToCents` (R1: quantity × price → cents), `quantityForCents` (R2: cents ÷ price → quantity), `impliedPrice` (R3: cents ÷ quantity → price, on absolute values), `allocateLargestRemainder` (R4), `scaleQty` (R5: split scaling), `fixed8ToCents`, `centsToFixed8`, `parseLooseDecimal`, `fixed8FromJsonNumber`, `percentToPpm`, `ppmToPercent`, `amountMismatch` (the 5-cent check shared by the trade preview and the engine), `RECONCILE_TOLERANCE_CENTS = 100n` and `matchesToDollar`. Rates (returns, ratios) are the only floats: computed, never stored, never fed back into cents.
2. **Rounding, stated once.** Half away from zero (`roundDivHalfAway`) at exactly these sites: R1, R2, R3, R5, `fixed8ToCents`, and the lot engine's basis consumption `roundDivHalfAway(basis × take, quantity)`. Cents split across pieces (proceeds across lots, class splits of a value) use largest remainder (R4). Every other integer division (basis points, ppm, target cents, pro-rata gain estimates, averages, monthly equivalents) keeps Phase 2's truncation toward zero. This is the one stated exception to the Phase 2 rule.
3. **Enums** (`packages/shared/src/enums.ts` is the single source; Drizzle text columns mirror them): `SecurityType = stock | etf | fund | bond | cash | cd | other`; `AssetClass = us_stock | intl_stock | bonds | cash | real_estate | other`; `PriceSource = provider | implied | manual` (also `security.price_mode`); `HoldingSource = simplefin | csv | manual`; `InvestmentTxnType = buy | sell | dividend | interest | reinvest | fee | split | transfer`; `InvestmentTxnSource = csv | manual | simplefin | ofx` (only the first two are written in Phase 3); `TransferDirection = in | out`; `LotMethod = fifo | specific`; `LotOpenedBy = buy | reinvest | transfer_in`; `GainTerm = short | long | unknown | none`; `DisposalKind = sell | transfer_out | unmatched_sell`; `InvestmentFidelity = balance_only | positions | full_history` (null for non-investment account types; there is no `none` member); `CashInference = auto | off`; `InvestmentIncomeFeed = off | link | create`; `CsvKind = bank | positions | trades`; `ValuationBasis = snapshot | lots | positions | balance | none`; `HoldingPriceSource = provider | implied | manual | statement | txn | cash`; `PriceStatus = statement | current | stale | implied | manual | cash | carried | missing` (the prices design's `fixed` is spelled `cash`); `LineKind = position | cash | cash_plug | carried_flows`; `InvestmentPeriod = 1w | 1m | 3m | 6m | ytd | 1y | 3y | 5y | all | custom` with one `periodStart`; `ReturnMethod = true_twr | modified_dietz`; `ExternalFlowKind = cash | securities | opening_value`; `ExternalFlowSource = ledger | investment_txn | engine`; `ReturnFailureReason` and `EngineWarningCode` per the lots design (`oversell`, `pick_unavailable`, `basis_unknown`, `stale_price`, …); `PriceProviderId = tiingo`; `PriceFetchStatus = ok | not_found | error | rate_limited`; `ReconcileRowStatus` per the prices design. Extended: `TransactionSource += investment`; `JobType += simplefin.reprocess_holdings, prices.fetch, investments.valuation, investments.income_feed`; `AuditEntity += security, investment_txn, holding_snapshot, allocation_target, lot_pick, account_reconciliation, account`; `ImportLogKind += csv.positions, csv.trades, simplefin.reprocess, prices.fetch`.

plus the prices columns `always_price`, `price_fetched_at`, `price_fetch_status`, `price_fetch_message`, `price_fetch_failures`, `price_next_attempt_at`, `price_backfilled_from`; partial unique indexes on ticker, cusip, isin and one cash security per currency; the normalized-name expression index; `security_cash_shape` CHECK), `security_alias` (imports), `security_allocation` (views: `(security_id, asset_class)` PK, `weight_bp` 1..10000), `price` (imports DDL with `close >= 0` and `source`, plus `adj_close`, `provider`, `suspect`, `fetched_at`, `updated_at` and the `price_provider_shape` CHECK; unique `(security_id, date)`), `holding_snapshot` (imports DDL: nullable `quantity` for dollar-only positions, `price`, `value_cents`, `reported_cost_basis_cents`, `source`, `import_log_id`; unique `(account_id, security_id, date)`), `investment_txn` (imports DDL and named CHECKs with these settlements: `sequence`; `fee_cents NOT NULL DEFAULT 0`; `amount_cents NOT NULL` signed cash effect; unsigned `quantity` plus `transfer_direction`; `split_num`/`split_den`; `cost_basis_cents`/`acquired_date` on transfers in; `transfer_group_id` + `counterparty_account_id`; **`ledger_transaction_id`** with a partial unique index; `external_id` `csvid:`/`inv:`; `source`; `import_log_id`; `raw_description`, `memo`, `user_edited`; **no** `lot_selection` column; plus the `(account_id, type, date)` index), `lot` (uuid PK; `opened_by_txn_id` FK investment_txn CASCADE; `ordinal`; unique `(opened_by_txn_id, ordinal)`; `origin_lot_id` nullable self-FK SET NULL; `account_id`, `security_id`, `opened_by`, `acquired_date`, `original_quantity`, `quantity`, `original_cost_basis_cents`, `cost_basis_cents`, `basis_known`, `closed_date`, `rebuilt_at`), `lot_pick` (`id`, `sell_txn_id` FK investment_txn CASCADE, `lot_id` FK lot CASCADE, `quantity > 0`, `position`, `created_at`; unique `(sell_txn_id, lot_id)`), `lot_disposal` (lots DDL incl. nullable `lot_id`, `kind`, `term`, `gain_cents`, `basis_known`; unique on `(close_txn_id, COALESCE(lot_id, '00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000'))`), `allocation_target` (`asset_class` PK, `target_bp`), `account_reconciliation` (prices DDL: several rows per account and date; `rows` jsonb of `ReconcileRowView`; `accepted`, `holding_snapshot_date`). Alterations: `account` gains `lot_method` (default `fifo`), `cash_inference` (default **`auto`**), `investment_income_feed` (default `off`) and **no** `investment_history`; `household_setting` gains `benchmark_security_id`, `investment_income_category_id`, `rebalance_band_bp` (default 500, 0..5000), `rebalance_min_trade_cents` (default 10000) and **no** `dividends_as_income`; `connection` gains `csv_kind` (default `bank`). No data backfill of any kind.
5. **Lots are a derived cache** rebuilt per account inside the writing transaction (`rebuildLots(tx, accountId)`, out-leg accounts of a transfer group first); lot ids are stable by `(opened_by_txn_id, ordinal)`; a transfer-in leg receives one lot per source lot (ordinals 0..n−1, `origin_lot_id`), or one lot with `basis_known = false` when no counterparty exists. Unknown basis is `basis_known = false` with basis 0 in the table and the engine, `null` on the wire. Specific identification uses `lot_pick` rows (Σ picks ≤ sale, FIFO for the remainder, `needs_lot_pick` warning when the method is `specific` and no picks exist); `CreateInvestmentTxnBody.lotPicks` writes them in the same request. Realized gains are read from `lot_disposal`; proceeds split across lots by largest remainder; split scaling R5 per lot without a residual fix (the 0.001-share position tolerance absorbs it). Same-day order is `(date, type rank, sequence, id)` with rank `split < transfer in < buy < reinvest < dividend/interest < fee < sell < transfer out`; `rowOrder` only orders same-rank rows.
6. **Reinvested dividends are two rows**: a `dividend` (income, cash +) and a `reinvest` (`amount_cents ≤ 0`, a purchase, cash −). The engine treats `reinvest` as a buy with basis `−amount` and never as income; `reinvestedCents` per month = Σ `−amount` of reinvest rows. Trade mappings carry `reinvestRows` (`separate` default, `combined` per institution) so either file convention lands as two rows; a reinvest-looking row without a quantity is income only.
7. **Fidelity is derived, never stored**: `accountFidelities(db, accountIds)` in `packages/db/src/services/accountFidelity.ts` = `hasTrades` / `hasSnapshots` plus the engine's `checkPositions` (0.001-share tolerance; a mismatch between the lots and the latest snapshot yields `positions` with a note). The check skips the cash security, securities with `asset_class = 'cash'` and dollar-only rows. `AccountView.investment = { fidelity, fidelityNote, positionsMatch, lastSnapshotDate, lastSnapshotSource, lastTradeDate, securityCount, needsReviewCount, lastReconciliation }` (null for non-investment types), plus `lotMethod`, `cashInference`, `investmentIncomeFeed` on the account.
8. **One valuation chain** `valueAccountOn` (prices design, `packages/shared/src/valuation.ts`) defines an investment account's value on a date for every consumer (Portfolio, Holdings, Allocation, the dashboard investments block, the reconcile preview, day and period change, and the performance engine's valuation points for non-full accounts): lots (only when derived fidelity is `full_history` and the latest trade checkpoint is newer than the latest snapshot) → the latest holding snapshot on or before the date, repriced after its date → the reported balance → `none`. Cash is anchored the same way for every basis: `anchor` = latest reported `balance_snapshot` (source ≠ `computed`) on or before the date; `cash_plug = anchor.balance − Σ positions priced at anchor.date`, carried forward; a positive plug is a `cash_plug` line labelled "Cash (from balance)", a negative one is a `negative_residual` warning, never negative cash; transfers after the anchor are carried as cash (`carried_flows`). `cash_inference = off` disables the plug and the carried flows for that account; the reconcile preview computes both settings. Holding value: the snapshot's own value on its date, `mulToCents(quantity, close)` when a price strictly newer than the snapshot exists; dollar-only rows always at the snapshot value. Cash securities are valued at 1 and never priced.
9. **Computed balance rows.** A worker job `investments.valuation` writes `balance_snapshot` rows with `source = 'computed'` from the chain (dates from the last reported row through the expected price date; `ON CONFLICT … WHERE source = 'computed'`, never overwriting a reported row) after every `prices.fetch` run, positions commit, manual snapshot and reconcile commit. Phase 2's net worth stays on `balance_snapshot` unchanged and equals the Portfolio on every date; the engine excludes computed rows from cash anchors.
10. **Prices.** Provider interface in `packages/shared/src/prices.ts` (pure parsing, symbol mapping, suspect rule, `expectedPriceDate` on the America/New_York clock via `localClockInTimeZone` and the NYSE calendar in `marketCalendar.ts`) with one adapter `apps/worker/src/prices/tiingo.ts`. `PRICE_PROVIDER = tiingo | none` (default `none`, which keeps the app correct on implied and manual prices and never queues a fetch); `TIINGO_API_KEY` from env or a secret file, never the database. The job `prices.fetch { reason: holdings | nightly | manual }` runs once a night after the US close in the household timezone plus one conditional catch-up, for held securities, the benchmark, `always_price` securities and any security with a `lot_disposal` dated within 400 days; held = `listHeldSecurityIds(db)`; only `price_mode = 'provider'` is fetched using `provider_symbol ?? ticker`; budget 45 requests per run spaced 1.5 s, 20-minute wall-clock cap, continuations deduped per root job, 3-day overlap, per-security exponential backoff, 3 attempts per symbol, `Retry-After` capped at 120 s; failures are recorded on `security.price_fetch_*` and shown by `GET /api/prices/status` and the Prices page (no alert rows). Every price refresh goes through `requestPriceRefresh` (which uses the new `enqueueJob` `payload.dedupeKey`). Price precedence `manual > provider > implied` through one `upsertPrices`; implied prices are written for every valued position lacking a provider or manual price by `rebuildImpliedPrices(tx, { securityIds, dates })` (deterministic tie rule: largest `value_cents`, then `account_id`). Adjusted closes are a column; the benchmark uses them when present (`adjusted: boolean` reported, `priceOnly` in the UI otherwise). User-facing staleness is counted in trading days against the expected close and applies only to provider-mode securities; the engine's 7-day `stale_price` stays an engine warning.
11. **External flows** come from one loader `loadExternalFlows(db, accountIds, from, to)`: posted ledger `is_transfer` rows ∪ `investment_txn` cash transfers (a cash row is dropped when `ledger_transaction_id` is set or an equal-amount ledger transfer within 3 days exists) ∪ securities transfers at market ∪ opening values of accounts entering after `from`; each flow carries `accountId`, `kind` and `source`. Start-of-day timing; true TWR (chain-linked sub-periods) for full-history accounts, Modified Dietz otherwise, the method named in the output; XIRR per Microsoft's definition with a deterministic bracket and bisection (tolerance 1e-10), typed failure reasons; annualized only when the range is ≥ 365 days; the benchmark is a shadow portfolio buying `cents ÷ close` units with every flow.
12. **Day change** = price movement on today's quantities (balance-only accounts excluded with a reason; the total marked partial). **Period change** = one computation: `changeCents`, `netFlowsCents` (external flows + opening values), `growthCents = change − netFlows`, plus `incomeCents` and `uncategorizedRows`; `flows_not_in_value` reason when a positions-basis account with cash inference off has flows in the window. The views design's "why did it change" attribution (price effect + quantity effect + residual + flows, summing exactly to Δ) stays for arbitrary windows.
13. **Income.** One route `GET /api/investments/income` (views design) built on the engine's pure `incomeByMonth(txns)`; months carry dividends, interest, fees and `reinvestedCents`. The feed into the plan is per account (`investment_income_feed`): `link` categorizes the bank twin of a dividend/interest row into `household_setting.investment_income_category_id` (an income-bucket category; unlocked rows only, like the rules engine); `create` adds synthetic ledger rows `source = 'investment'` keyed `inv:<investment_txn id>` for accounts without a bank feed and an upgrade pass replaces them when the bank row arrives; reinvested dividends are never fed; feed rows are excluded from transfer pairing and recurring detection; the Phase 2 plan loaders read feed-category rows from off-budget accounts. The job `investments.income_feed` runs after syncs, trade imports, feed-setting changes and the daily tick.
14. **Reconciliation** (the gate): the prices design's `account_reconciliation` table, `POST /api/reconcile/preview` (readOnly, writes nothing), `POST /api/reconcile/commit` (owner; always records the attempt; with `accept = true` writes the statement as a `manual` holding snapshot, replaces that date's balance row, rebuilds implied prices, enqueues the valuation job, audits one batch), `GET /api/reconcile/history`, `DELETE /api/reconcile/:id`; audit entity `account_reconciliation`. `matchesToDollar = |diff| < 100` cents everywhere (imports preview, reconcile, dashboard); a difference of 0 is shown as "exact". Positions imports write `balance_snapshot = statementTotalCents ?? Σ positions` for accounts without a SimpleFIN feed (`PositionsCommitBody` and `ManualPositionsBody` gain optional `statementTotalCents`) and only on request for SimpleFIN-fed ones. The sync's balance upsert gains `WHERE source <> 'manual'` so a hand-checked statement survives the next sync; snapshot precedence `manual > csv > simplefin` applies to holding and balance rows alike.
15. **Contributions.** The ingest-time classifier marks inflows into investment-type accounts `is_transfer = true` (unpaired transfers, as the rules engine may); trade-file cash rows become `investment_txn` cash transfers and, for accounts without a SimpleFIN connection, also ledger rows (`is_transfer`, Phase 1 ids) linked by `ledger_transaction_id`; a manual cash transfer into such an account creates the ledger row the same way, so hand-entered 401(k) deferrals reach savings goals. Investment-type accounts default `on_budget = false` at creation (no migration backfill; the Accounts page offers a one-click move); Phase 2's `loadActuals`, `loadUncategorized` and `countUncategorized` gain `account.on_budget = true OR category = feed category`; `loadGoalNets` is unchanged.
16. **Allocation.** `security.asset_class` is the primary class; `security_allocation` splits (Σ 10000 bp, primary must be a split class, cash securities never split) refine blended funds; household targets in `allocation_target` replaced as a whole set (`400 allocation_sum`); allocation covers positions and full-history accounts only (balance-only accounts shown as unclassified); drift against `rebalance_band_bp`; the deterministic greedy rebalance (tax rank `pre_tax = hsa = roth < none < taxable`, then value descending; per-account sells fund buys; never moves money across accounts; minimum trade `rebalance_min_trade_cents`; pro-rata gain estimate on taxable sells). Holdings rows per security across accounts carry cost basis with coverage `full | partial | none` and `basisSource ∈ lots | statement`, the integer-ppm weighted expense ratio with `coveredBp`, and concentration warnings (stock ≥ 10% warn, ≥ 25% bad; a single fund ≥ 60% info).
17. **Routes and files** (one owner each): imports → `routes/securities.ts`, `routes/investmentTxns.ts`, additions to `routes/import.ts` (positions and trades preview/commit, `connection.csv_kind`); lots → `routes/performance.ts` (`POST /api/investments/performance` readOnly with `detail`, `GET /api/investments/lots`, `GET /api/investments/realized`, `PUT /api/investments/txns/:id/lot-picks`, `POST /api/investments/lots/rebuild`); prices → `routes/prices.ts` (`GET /api/prices/status`, `POST /api/prices/refresh`, `PUT/DELETE /api/prices/:securityId/:date`), `routes/valuation.ts` (`GET /api/valuation/summary`, `GET /api/valuation/series`), `routes/reconcile.ts`; views → `routes/investmentViews.ts` (`GET /api/investments/portfolio | allocation | holdings | performance | income | changes`, `PUT /api/investments/securities/:id/allocation`, `GET/PUT /api/investments/allocation-targets` is the imports area's). One `PATCH /api/investments/securities/:id` whose pricing fields call `updateSecurityPricing`; manual prices only at `PUT/DELETE /api/prices/:securityId/:date`. `asOf` is the date parameter on every GET; `accountIds` is comma-separated on GETs; the engine's POST keeps its body array and a test asserts the GET presentation equals the POST. View mappers live beside the routes under `apps/api/src/views/`.
18. **Jobs.** `enqueueJob` gains `payload.dedupeKey` (existing callers unchanged); the runner retries unless `err.permanent === true`; `investments.valuation` is chained from the end of every `prices.fetch` run and enqueued (deduped) after positions commits, manual snapshots and reconcile commits; `DAILY_JOB_TYPES = recurring.detect, investments.income_feed, plan.close_month`; `schedulePriceJobs` runs separately on the household-local clock; `simplefin.reprocess_holdings` replays stored `import_log` payloads through the holdings parser. The sync applies holdings per account (`account.holdings` and five `extra` shapes, raw payload kept, `holdings.unrecognized` / `partial` reported per account, never dated by the clock), then `linkUnlinkedInvestmentTxns`, then enqueues the income feed.
19. **Viewer role.** Every read is a GET (or a `readOnly` POST, the cash-flow precedent); mutations are refused by the auth plugin; `AuthStatus` gains `role`; the web hides editors.

## Deviations from the spec (flagged)

- OFX/QFX import deferred to Polish (build list over data-sources table).
- Rounding half away from zero at the six named sites (decision 2), an exception to Phase 2's truncation rule.
- `investment_txn.type` gains `interest`; `security.type` gains `other`; columns beyond the spec rows on every table (idempotent imports, ordering, corporate actions, transfers, editable imported rows, fetch state, adjusted closes).
- Added tables `security_alias`, `security_allocation`, `lot_pick`, `lot_disposal`, `allocation_target`, `account_reconciliation`; added columns on `account`, `household_setting`, `connection`.
- `lot` is a derived cache with a nullable basis flag; cost basis from the statement is displayed but never used for gains.
- Implied prices are stored as price rows for every position lacking a provider or manual price, not only ticker-less funds; prices are fetched nightly plus a catch-up, and for the benchmark and `always_price` securities although not held.
- Cash inference (balance plug and carried transfer legs) is an addition, on by default, always labelled.
- Investment accounts default off-budget at creation; Phase 2's plan loaders exclude off-budget accounts except feed-category rows.
- Wash sales, return of capital and capital-gain distributions are not special-cased; multi-currency is not supported (a non-USD holding is a preview warning); money-weighted return for sub-year ranges is shown unannualized beside Excel's XIRR.
- The free price source (Tiingo) needs a free API key; its current terms and fund coverage must be confirmed by the owner at signup.

## Recorded during implementation

Decisions the build settled where the plan or a design left room, all consistent with the decisions above:

- Prices: a persistent 429 records `price_fetch_status = 'rate_limited'` on the security that hit it; a run in which every request died on the network writes no per-security backoff (so the queue's retry finds the same plan), a stated exception to prices design §3.11 step 4; the API and worker share one `PRICE_PROVIDER` parser; `GET /api/prices/:securityId` and `POST /api/prices/rebuild-implied` exist beyond decision 17's list; the valuation job is also requested after manual price writes, trade imports and manual investment transactions.
- Valuation: on the positions basis the cash plug is not anchored on a reported balance older than the snapshot in use (a `negative_residual` warning names the date instead); the period change reports `partial` and `missingAccountIds` when an account valued at the start cannot be valued at the end; the valuation job rewrites computed balance rows from the earliest computed row forward, and dates between two older reported rows that never received computed rows stay unfilled (an owner call on widening the window to the first data date).
- Reconciliation: a total-only accept (no rows) records a manual balance for the date and never deletes the positions; the preview computes the alternative cash-inference reading in the off → auto direction.
- Transactions: a ledger row is treated as derived from an investment cash transfer only when the service wrote it (`inv:<id>` external id or the same import log), so a bank row merely linked to a transfer is never rewritten or deleted; paired cash transfers mirror amount and date edits onto the counterpart leg.
- Imports: the trade-action classifier reads the trade verbs (bought, sold, reinvest) before the income words, so a fund whose name contains "dividend" or "interest" still imports as a trade; an income word decides only when no trade verb is present or the row has no quantity (imports design §3.8 lists the older order).
- Services: the sync's balance upsert yields to both `manual` and `csv` balance rows (the full rank of decision 14); a manual or implied price replacing a provider row clears `adj_close`; migration `0003_security_name_index` aligns the normalized-name index with the resolver's predicate; the performance total excludes accounts with no valuation in the window and names them in `excludedAccountIds`; `flows_not_in_value` also covers shares received in kind after the last statement.
- Views and web: `PUT /api/investments/securities/:id/allocation` lives in `routes/securities.ts`; the GET performance presentation shows an unpriced benchmark with reason `no_benchmark_price`; `PATCH /api/accounts/:id` audits `lotMethod` and `cashInference` changes as entity `account`; the Investments sub-navigation gains a Securities entry; the cross-account investment-transactions list page is not built (the per-account Trades tab covers it); allocation bars are a CSS component.
